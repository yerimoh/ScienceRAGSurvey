How a scientific RAG system works

The survey organizes the field not as a grid of cells but as the pipeline a system runs: what knowledge it retrieves (the substrate), how it constructs, retrieves, generates, and verifies, and what objective it produces, scored by evaluation. Science specializes every stage.

What's next

Across the grid, one message stands out. Capability piles up where the substrate is textual and the task can be scored automatically, and it thins toward non-textual evidence and externally verified discovery, the very abilities an autonomous scientific agent leans on most. The work ahead is less a search for better retrievers than making more of science retrievable and more of its outputs verifiable.
