How a scientific RAG system works

The survey organizes the field not as a grid of cells but as the pipeline a system runs: what knowledge it retrieves (the substrate), how it constructs, retrieves, generates, and verifies, and what objective it produces — scored by evaluation. Science specializes every stage.

Every stage links to its explanation. Capability concentrates on the Textual substrate and automatically-checkable tasks, and thins toward non-textual, externally-verified discovery.
