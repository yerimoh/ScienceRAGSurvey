#### 📌 TL;DR
An internationally coordinated global biodiversity information facility (GBIF) that integrates and openly provides species occurrence records collected from diverse institutions worldwide.

#### 🎯 Background
**Limitations of existing infrastructure**
- Global biodiversity data was scattered across numerous institutions (natural history museums, universities, government agencies), making global-scale research difficult
- A global data-access infrastructure was needed for biodiversity conservation, climate change response, and ecological research

**Why this system is needed**
- Open infrastructure to support biodiversity research, conservation, and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
- Enables researchers, policymakers, and the general public worldwide to freely access biodiversity data
- Standardizes and integrates data from diverse data providers (natural history museums, citizen science, remote sensing, etc.)

#### 🔨 Architecture
GBIF is an open data infrastructure based on international collaboration, integrating species occurrence records submitted by thousands of institutions worldwide under the Darwin Core standard. It provides data access through the GBIF.org portal and a REST API.
