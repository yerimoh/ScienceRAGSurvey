#### 📌 TL;DR
ICSD is an inorganic crystal structure DB operated by FIZ Karlsruhe since 1978. Together with the CSD, it forms one of the two pillars of crystallographic infrastructure and is a standard resource for materials science and solid-state chemistry research.

#### 🎯 Background
**Limitations of existing infrastructure**
- Inorganic and metallic crystal structure data were scattered across many journals, making systematic access difficult
- While the CSD specializes in organic and organometallic compounds, a DB dedicated to purely inorganic structures was needed
**Why this system is needed**
- A standard repository of crystal structures for inorganic materials such as oxides, nitrides, alloys, and ceramics
- Essential as an initial-structure reference for materials design and DFT calculations

#### 🔨 Architecture
It collects published inorganic crystal structures and stores them in a standardized format. Operated by FIZ Karlsruhe (Leibniz Institute for Information Infrastructure in Karlsruhe, Germany). Includes chemical composition, space group, lattice parameters, atomic coordinates, and more.

#### ⚠️ Limitations
- Being subscription-based, it is not directly accessible to open-science RAG systems
- The domain boundary between the CSD (organic) and ICSD (inorganic) overlaps for organometallic compounds
- High cost limits access for small institutions
