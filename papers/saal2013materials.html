📌 TL;DR

An open quantum materials database built by the Wolverton group at Northwestern University, providing more than 200,000 DFT-computed crystal structures and formation energies free of charge for use in materials design and discovery.

🎯 Background

Limitations of existing infrastructure

  • Materials-property data generated by high-throughput DFT calculations were not made public, making reproduction and reuse impossible
  • Lack of large-scale thermodynamic data for practical materials problems such as batteries, lightweight alloys, and coatings
  • Experiment-based exploration alone cannot cover the vast compositional space

Why this system is needed

  • Performs extensive DFT calculations using compounds recorded in the ICSD (Inorganic Crystal Structure Database) plus decorations of common crystal structures
  • Directly applied to five materials problems, including Li-air batteries, Li-ion battery anodes/cathodes, and Mg alloys
  • Enables prediction of new stable compounds via thermodynamic stability (GCLP) calculations

🔨 Architecture

OQMD computes the total energy and formation energy of crystal structures through DFT calculations using VASP (GGA-PBE functional). It is composed of ICSD-recorded structures and element-wise decorations of common prototype structures. Through global constrained linear programming (GCLP), it can predict the thermodynamic stability of arbitrary compositions.

📤 Data formats

  • DFT-computed crystal structures (total energy, optimized lattice constants)
  • Formation energy (eV/atom)
  • Thermodynamic stability (convex hull analysis)
  • Band gap (for some compounds)

📊 Key statistics (as reported in the paper)

Item Value
Total DFT-computed crystal structures 200,000+ (as of the 2013 paper)
Comparison with experimental formation energies Validated against 1,670 crystals (kirklin2015)
Data availability Freely public (http://oqmd.org)
Applied materials problems 5 (battery anode/cathode/coating, Mg alloys, ML prediction)

⚠️ Limitations

  • More than 200,000 as of paper publication (2013); expanded to about 300,000 as reported in the 2015 kirklin2015 paper
  • Formation-energy inaccuracy due to systematic errors of the GGA functional (average error of about 0.1 eV/atom versus experimental values)
  • Dynamical stability and thermal/entropic effects are not included
  • Reduced accuracy for certain elemental systems (transition-metal oxides, f-electron systems) when DFT+U is not applied
