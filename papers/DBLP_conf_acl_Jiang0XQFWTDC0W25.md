# HyKGE: Hypothesis Knowledge Graph Enhanced RAG
> ACL 2025 | Method | medical

## TL;DR
A KG-based RAG that, to compensate for short and incomplete medical queries, uses the hypothesis output (Hypothesis Output, HO) first generated by an LLM as a retrieval seed, retrieves multi-hop reasoning chains over a fused curated medical knowledge graph, filters out noise via HO fragment-level reranking, and synthesizes a comprehensive answer. It injects external KG knowledge with only 2 LLM calls.

## Architecture (HyKGE Architecture)
- **LLM Hypothesis Output (HO):** Generates step-by-step hypotheses for the query with a "medical expert" prompt, drawing out entities not present in the query to richly seed retrieval.
- **Fused curated medical KG:** A fusion of CMeKG + CPubMed-KG + Disease-KG (about 1.289M entities, about 3.569M relations). Entity descriptions are augmented from Wikipedia/Baidu/Medical Baike.
- **NER and linking:** Extracts entities from query⊕HO with W2NER, and links them to KG nodes via GTE embedding similarity (δ=0.7).
- **Multi-hop retrieval:** Collects Path / Co-ancestor / Co-occurrence reasoning chains within k=3 hops + entity descriptions.
- **HO Fragment Granularity-aware Reranking:** Chunks query⊕HO into fragments (window 10 / overlap 4), scores and prunes chains with a BGE reranker (topK=10) — aligning the density of retrieved knowledge and the hypothesis.

## Pipeline (inference)
1. Query → LLM hypothesis output HO.
2. NER(query⊕HO) → GTE linking to fix KG entities.
3. Retrieve k=3 multi-hop reasoning chains.
4. BGE reranking based on HO fragments → topK=10 chains.
5. Serialize chains with arrows + combine descriptions → LLM Reader generates a comprehensive answer.

## Key results
Outperforms 8 baselines on Chinese medical QA (MMCU-Medical, CMB-Exam, CMB-Clin). For example, MMCU-Medical EM (GPT-3.5) Base 43.52 → **HyKGE 49.65**, PCR 50.55 → **57.82**. Interpretability (ACJ/PPL/ROUGE-R) also improves substantially. With 2 LLM calls and an average of 19.76 seconds, it is more efficient than multi-stage baselines. Ablation: removing HO causes the largest drop (49.65→41.08).

## Limitations
- Slight additional time due to noise filtering.
- Limited to Chinese medical datasets (not validated on other languages or domains).
- Performance depends on KG completeness, and since the original KG lacks entity descriptions, external augmentation is required.
