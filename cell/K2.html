Curated graphs of scientific relations, UMLS, PrimeKG, DRKG, STRING, KEGG, citation graphs. Reached by entity linking and traversal.

Biomedical knowledge graph-optimized prompt generation for large language models ↗

KG-RAG, Bioinformatics, 2024

Extracts a disease-centric minimal-schema subgraph from the SPOKE biomedical KG (41 databases, ~42M nodes) and injects only the key triples via embedding pruning. Versus Cypher-RAG: 54% fewer tokens and retrieval accuracy from 75% to 97%.

MindMap: Knowledge Graph Prompting Sparks Graph of Thoughts in Large Language Models ↗

MindMap, ACL, 2024

Diagnostic QA that retrieves path/neighbor evidence subgraphs from a curated clinical KG (disease-symptom-drug) and integrates them along graph-of-thoughts reasoning paths. With a GPT-3.5 backbone it beats GPT-4 on some baselines.

KG-Rank: Enhancing Large Language Models for Medical QA with Knowledge Graphs and Ranking Techniques ↗

KG-Rank, BioNLP@ACL, 2024

Retrieves one-hop UMLS triples for the question's entities and narrows them by ranking/re-ranking (similarity, answer expansion, MMR, MedCPT) to synthesize a long-form medical answer. No fine-tuning needed.

HyKGE: A Hypothesis Knowledge Graph Enhanced RAG Framework for Accurate and Reliable Medical LLMs Responses ↗

HyKGE, ACL, 2025

Seeds retrieval with an LLM hypothesis (HO) to fetch multi-hop reasoning chains from a fused curated medical KG (CMeKG, CPubMed-KG, Disease-KG; ~1.29M entities), filtering noise by HO-fragment reranking to synthesize a comprehensive answer in 2 LLM calls.

KGARevion: An AI Agent for Knowledge-Intensive Biomedical QA ↗

KGARevion, ICLR, 2025

An agent loop of generating candidate triples, verifying them against a KG (PrimeKG/biokg), revising, and answering. Verifying the LLM's latent knowledge against the KG removes factual errors and improves medical-QA reliability.

CG-RAG: Research Question Answering by Citation Graph Retrieval-Augmented LLMs ↗

CG-RAG, SIGIR, 2025

Proposes LeSeGR, which retrieves over a paper citation graph: it links paper chunks by citation relations to fuse sparse and dense signals, and answers research queries grounded in the retrieved evidence.

GraPPI: A Retrieve-Divide-Solve GraphRAG Framework for Large-scale Protein-protein Interaction Exploration ↗

GraPPI, NAACL, 2025

Traverses the curated STRING PPI graph (18,767 nodes / 2.95M edges) with a Retrieve-Divide-Solve scheme, splitting each path into edges, explaining them in parallel, then synthesizing and re-ranking at the path level to generate drug-target signaling-pathway analyses.
