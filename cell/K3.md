- **[Overview of the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6) experimental design and organization](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-9-1937-2016)** — Geoscientific Model Development · 2016 — CMIP6 design paper; petabyte-scale (~20 PB realized) climate model simulations from 49 groups
- **[Preparing a Collection of Radiology Examinations for Distribution and Retrieval (IU-Xray / Open-i)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jamia/ocv080)** — IU-Xray · JAMIA · 2016 — 3,955 chest X-ray reports + 7,470 DICOM images; Indiana University chest X-ray collection (IU-Xray)
