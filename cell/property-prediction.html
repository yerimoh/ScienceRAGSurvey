O3 📈 Property Prediction

Property prediction specifies an entity and a target property and scores the prediction against a held-out measurement. Curated-database recovery over the Structured-entity substrate covers molecular property and toxicity in MoleculeNet and the Therapeutics Data Commons, and protein function against time-delayed annotations in CAFA. Measured-response recovery covers gene-perturbation response in GEARS and PerturBench. Link prediction over the Relational substrate scores held-out biomedical edges in OpenBioLink and OGB-biokg, and spectrum-to-molecule elucidation over the Perceptual substrate scores recovered structures in MassSpecGym. A held-out reference contains only what has already been measured, so a correct prediction outside it is scored as wrong, and recovery certifies fit rather than mechanism, since a strong fit can be interpolation.
