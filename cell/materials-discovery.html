O3 💎 Materials Discovery

Materials discovery sets a design target such as a stable crystal or a catalytic surface and scores it by re-running a physics simulator over the Structured-entity substrate of crystal and surface records. Matbench Discovery scores thermodynamic stability against DFT-relaxed ground truth, ADsorbML re-evaluates each adsorption structure with a DFT single point, MLIP Arena tests dynamical consistency beyond static references, and ScienceAgentBench executes generated code against task success criteria. A simulator confirms candidates far faster than laboratory screening, but it cannot judge the limits of its own functionals, kinetics, or synthesis feasibility, bounding what a passing score establishes.
