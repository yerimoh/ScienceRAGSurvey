O Operational Objective

A system's operational objective is the task it must serve, and each such task is operationalized by a benchmark that fixes the query, corpus, and ground truth of the pipeline equation.

O3

Discovery

details & 13 systems →

Propose an output the corpus does not contain, judged by an external verifier. (Property Prediction, Molecular Design, Materials Discovery, Hypothesis Generation.)

Property Prediction

Property prediction specifies an entity and a target property and scores the prediction against a held-out measurement.

MoleculeNetTDCCAFAGEARSPerturBenchOpenBioLinkOGB-biokgMassSpecGym

Molecular Design

Molecular design sets a target such as a protein pocket or a property profile and scores a proposed molecule the corpus does not contain.

PMOCrossDocked2020

Materials Discovery

Materials discovery sets a design target such as a stable crystal or a catalytic surface and scores it by re-running a physics simulator over the Structured-entity substrate of crystal and surface records.

Matbench DiscoveryADsorbMLMLIP ArenaScienceAgentBench

Hypothesis Generation

Hypothesis generation provides a research context and scores a proposed idea, mechanism, or pathway that has no stored answer to match, recovering it against a held-out reference rather than a lexical gold.
