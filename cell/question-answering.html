O1 Question Answering

Question answering poses a question with a determinate answer and scores the returned answer against a gold label. Text-only benchmarks cover clinical and biomedical questions in MIRAGE, a suite of five medical QA sets, and PubMedQA, yes/no/maybe questions over abstracts, with LitQA2 requiring answers found only in paper bodies. Broader science is covered by MaScQA for materials-exam problems scored against official answer keys and SciQ for crowd-sourced multiple-choice questions across biology, chemistry, earth science, and physics. Retrieval over a Relational source appears in SciQA, which answers SPARQL queries over the Open Research Knowledge Graph against a gold answer set. Cross-modal question answering pairs a question with non-text evidence over the Perceptual substrate, as in the pathology-image benchmarks PathMMU and Path-VQA. Question answering is the most saturated task in scientific RAG, because its gold labels make scoring automatic, but that same fixed gold cannot separate genuine retrieval from parametric recall, and overlap between corpus and benchmark inflates the reported score.
