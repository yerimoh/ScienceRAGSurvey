M Method: The Scientific RAG Pipeline

A scientific RAG system solves the tasks of the Operational Objective axis by turning a query into an output through the pipeline of the overview figure, and we organize its methods by the stage at which they act.

Construction Retrieval Integration Verification
M1

🏗️ Knowledge Construction and Indexing

full detail →

Construction is the offline step that builds the index.

M2

🔍 Retrieval

full detail →

Retrieval decides which stored knowledge the system can actually use, since a generator reasons only over what retrieval returns.

M3

🧩 Integration and Generation

full detail →

Once evidence is retrieved, integration decides how it enters generation, and this is where the reranker and the generator of the pipeline equation act.

M4

✅ Verification

full detail →

Generation produces an output, but in science that output is trusted only to the extent it has been checked against something beyond the retrieved evidence.
