Txt

Textual

details & 22 systems →

The textual substrate is the prose record of science, the papers, abstracts, clinical notes, and guideline documents in which findings are written down.

General-purpose and domain literature

General-purpose corpora carry no disciplinary boundary, so a single pipeline serves many fields.

CrossrefORCIDOpenAlexPubMedS2ORCScopusWeb of ScienceDimensionsCOREEurope PMCEmbaseNASA ADSINSPIRE-HEPGeoRefIPCC assessment reportsMedCorpMatSciKB

Preprint literature

Preprints trade peer review for recency, offering the most current view of active science.

bioRxivmedRxivChemRxivEarthArXivOSF Preprints

Institution-private corpora

Institution-private corpora are collaboration wikis, logbooks, and internal notes that share the same textual substrate but stay behind access restrictions.

MITRADUNE-GPT
Rel

Relational

details & 8 systems →

The relational substrate is the curated web of scientific relations, the graphs that record how genes, diseases, drugs, and concepts connect.

Curated concept-relation graphs

These are expert-curated graphs of asserted relations among canonical scientific entities.

UMLSSNOMED CTICD-10/11PrimeKGDRKGSTITCHKEGGReactomeGene OntologyOMIMDisGeNETClinVar

Literature-derived graphs

The same primary literature, once its citations and shared entities are linked, forms a graph rather than a set of passages.

OpenAlexS2ORC
Str

Structured-entity

details & 7 systems →

The structured-entity substrate is the objects of science themselves, the molecules, crystals, and materials held as data rather than described in words.

Molecule and structure libraries

These are libraries of molecular and 3D structure.

ChEMBLZINCPubChemPDBAlphaFold DBCrossDocked2020

Property databases

These are property tables built from large-scale computation or measurement.

Materials ProjectOQMDAFLOWOpen CatalystQM9QMugsBindingDB
Prc

Perceptual

details & 9 systems →

The perceptual substrate is the raw output of scientific instruments, the images, spectra, and signals recorded straight from measurement.

Medical and biological imaging

Medical imaging dominates this modality.

MIMIC-CXRIU-XrayADNIOASISABIDETCGAHuman Protein Atlas

Spectral libraries

Spectral libraries store the raw signal traces that identify a molecule.

MassBankGNPSHMDB

Instrument arrays and time series

Arrays and time series are the numerical readings streamed from instruments, and no standard encoder for them exists yet.

SDSSGaiaMASTNASA EOSDISCMIP6NOAA NCEIUSGS EROSCERN Open Data
