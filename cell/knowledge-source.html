Textual

details & 22 systems →

The textual substrate is the prose record of science, the papers, abstracts, clinical notes, and guideline documents in which findings are written down.

General-purpose and domain literature

General-purpose corpora carry no disciplinary boundary, so a single pipeline serves many fields.
